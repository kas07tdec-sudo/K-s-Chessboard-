Designed with a mahogany wood aesthetic, native Web Audio API acoustic synthesis, custom AI levels, chess clocks, and full legal rules engine — deployed seamlessly on **Vercel**.

## ✨ Features

- **🧠 Multi-Level AI Engine:**
  - **Easy:** Casual play with randomized legal move selection.
  - **Medium:** Tactical material evaluation engine.
  - **Hard:** **Minimax Algorithm with Alpha-Beta Pruning** searching 3 moves ahead.
- **⏱️ Integrated Chess Clocks:** Blitz (3m), Rapid (10m), and Classical (15m) timers with ticking effects and "Lost on Time" game over alerts.
- **🖐️ Interactivity:** Native HTML5 Drag-and-Drop and click-to-move support.
- **👑 Rules & Check Engine:**
  - **Special Rules:** Castling (`O-O` / `O-O-O`) and En Passant pawn captures.
  - **Check & Checkmate Detection:** King threat highlights, stalemate handling, and victory modals.
- **💡 Real-Time Move Hint Engine:** Calculates and highlights the best move on the board.
- **🎨 Theme Engine:** Switch between **Classic Wood**, **Cyberpunk Neon**, and **Royal Marble** themes instantly.
- **⏪ Move Undo & Log:** Human-readable activity log with single-click move undo capabilities.
- **🔊 Acoustic Audio Engine:** Native Web Audio API sound synthesizer generating authentic wooden impact effects without external audio assets.
